TechLife Guide is a small, independent publication about everyday technology, home, health, and lifestyle decisions. We write practical, specific guides — the kind of thing you want before you spend money, change a habit, or fix something at home.

## Who runs this site

TechLife Guide is written and maintained by **Peng Jiang**, an engineer who spends far too much time reading spec sheets, testing cheap gadgets, and figuring out what actually works versus what is just marketed well. This site started as a place to write down those findings so friends would stop asking the same questions.

## How we make our content

We believe in being transparent about our process:

- **We start from real questions.** Every article begins with a specific question people actually search for, not a keyword stuffed into a template.
- **We research before we write.** Each guide is drafted with the help of AI tooling, then checked, edited, and trimmed for accuracy and usefulness. We remove filler, generic advice, and anything we could not stand behind.
- **We are honest about limits.** We are not doctors, licensed electricians, or financial advisors. Where a topic is safety- or health-critical, we point you to qualified professionals.

## What we are not

We do not take money to recommend products, and we do not publish sponsored rankings. If that changes, it will be labeled clearly.

## Corrections

If you spot something that is wrong, outdated, or could be explained better, please [contact us](/contact/). We would rather fix it than leave it up.
